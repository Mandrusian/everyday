Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

The short version. everyday. has no servers and no accounts. Your mail, tasks, news and everything the app learns stay in a database on your device. The app only talks to services you choose: your mail provider, news publishers, the weather service, and an AI provider if you add your own key. We never receive your data.

Who this covers

This policy covers the everyday. app for Mac and Windows (“the app”) and the website at everyday.mandrusia.tech (“the site”). everyday. is a free, open-source project maintained by Mandrusian (“we”, “us”). The source code is public at github.com/Mandrusian/everyday, so you can check everything described here.

What the app stores on your device

WhatWhere
Mail you sync (inbox messages from the last 7 to 90 days, your choice), summaries, labels and reply draftsA local database in ~/Library/Application Support/everyday on Mac, or your user profile on Windows
Tasks, saved stories, news sources, what you swipe and read, and the interests learned from itThe same local database
Mail passwords, Google sign-in tokens and AI keysThe macOS Keychain or Windows Credential Manager
Spoken audio for “Listen”, if you use a natural voiceA cache folder on your device, which you can clear in Settings → Voice
A log of every AI request: when, which model, which feature, the text sent and the tokens usedThe local database, shown in Settings → AI provider

You can delete all of it from Settings → Privacy, or by removing the app's folder. Removing a mail account in Settings → Accounts can also delete that account's stored mail.

Each of these services handles what it receives under its own privacy policy. Requests go directly from your device to them; nothing passes through us.

Google user data (Gmail)

If you connect Gmail with Google sign-in, the app uses the Gmail API to read, label and archive your mail and to send replies you approve. Data received from Google is used only to show you your mail and provide the app's features on your device. It is not sent to us, not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train any AI model. If you've added an AI key, the text of messages you choose to summarise or reply to is sent to that AI provider on your behalf, as described above. everyday.'s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

AI providers

You bring your own key, so your agreement is with that provider, and their terms decide how they keep or use what you send. Some providers keep inputs for a period, and some free tiers may use them to improve their services. Check your provider's terms, or use a local model if you'd rather nothing left your device.

What we don't do

There is no analytics, advertising, tracking, fingerprinting or crash reporting in the app or on the site. We don't sell or share personal information, because we never receive it.

The website

The site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. It loads no third-party scripts or fonts and sets no cookies. Your light or dark choice is saved in your own browser's local storage. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit; see the GitHub privacy statement. Downloads are served from GitHub Releases on the same terms.

Getting help

Support happens through GitHub issues, which are public. Don't post email contents, passwords, keys or other personal details there. If you need to report a security problem, use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on the repository instead.

Security

Passwords and keys are kept in the operating system's secure store, never in plain files. Mail is fetched over encrypted connections. Text from emails and web pages is treated as untrusted, and the AI can't send, archive or delete anything: only your button presses can.

Children

everyday. is not directed at children under 16 and we don't knowingly receive data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because your information stays on your device and with the services you chose, you control it directly: you can see, export or delete it in the app, and contact your mail or AI provider for what they hold. If you're in Australia, you can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner. If you're in the EU or UK, you can contact your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date, and the change will be visible in the repository's history.

Questions about this page? Open an issue at github.com/Mandrusian/everyday/issues. Issues are public, so don't include personal details there.
