Privacy

Draft. Have this checked before launch.

What everyday. is

A Mac app that runs entirely on your computer. There is no everyday. account and no everyday. server. The authors receive no data from the app.

Stored on your Mac

Your mail, tasks, news, preferences and what the app has learned are in a local database in ~/Library/Application Support/everyday. Mail passwords and AI keys are stored in the macOS Keychain. You can delete everything from Settings → Privacy.

Sent from your Mac to others

  • Your mail provider, to read, and (only when you approve) to send, archive or delete mail.
  • News publishers, when the app fetches their feeds and articles.
  • Open-Meteo, your city name and its coordinates, for the weather.
  • everyday.mandrusia.tech, once a day, to read a small public file that says whether a newer version exists. Nothing about you is sent, and you can turn it off in Settings → General.
  • The AI provider you choose, only if you add a key. Settings → AI provider → activity log shows every request and the exact text sent. You can limit this to subject lines and snippets.

Not collected

No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting is built in.
